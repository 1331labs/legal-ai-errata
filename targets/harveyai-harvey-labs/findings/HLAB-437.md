## Defect
Criterion C-022 in tasks/emerging-companies-venture-capital/draft-legal-opinion-for-series-b-preferred-stock-financing/task.json requires the solver to contrast the bylaws' quorum of a majority of the authorized number of directors (3 of 5) with what it calls the DGCL section 141(b) default of a majority of the total number of directors then in office (3 of 4 sitting directors), and to discuss the difference. C-024 requires a note that the statutory default is a majority of directors then in office, C-023 requires confirmation that quorum was met under either standard, and C-060 says the bylaws' wording may differ from the statutory default. Section 141(b) provides that a majority of the total number of directors constitutes a quorum; the words then in office do not appear in section 141(b), and the Court of Chancery, quoting the Delaware Supreme Court, has stated that total number of directors refers to directorships, not directors actually in office. The bylaws' wording therefore tracks the statutory default. An opinion that states the law correctly (quorum is 3 of 5 authorized seats under both the statute and the bylaws, and 3 directors attended) fails C-022 for not raising the supposed discrepancy, and because every criterion must be met it loses the task; C-024's PASS clause also asks it to note a "then in office" default that section 141(b) does not state.

Criterion C-020 (line 182) requires the issues memo to identify "that the SPA Schedule of Purchasers totals to $42,000,002" against the officer certificate's $42,000,000. The SPA's Schedule of Purchasers (documents/series-b-spa.docx, Exhibit A) rows sum to its stated total of $42,000,000 ($28,000,002 + $9,000,000 + $4,999,998), because Whitecliff's $2 rounding up is offset by Apex's $2 rounding down (833,333 × $6.00 = $4,999,998), so the officer certificate's aggregate figure of $42,000,000 agrees with the SPA. The $42,000,002 figure appears in the Investors' Rights Agreement (documents/investors-rights-agreement.docx, Exhibit A), which lists Apex at $5,000,000; the officer certificate's own Schedule of Series B Purchasers also lists Apex at $5,000,000, so its rows sum to $42,000,002 against its stated total of $42,000,000, and the board minutes state that Apex would invest $5,000,000. A memo that reads the SPA schedule correctly and locates the $2 difference where it is cannot satisfy C-020 as written.

## Objections considered
- Objection: The directorships reading rests on a Court of Chancery opinion quoting Crown EMAK, not on the Supreme Court opinion itself.
  Answer: The finding stands on the statutory text alone, because the criteria put the words "then in office" in quotation marks as the text of section 141(b) and the subsection does not contain them.
- Objection: The criteria only flag a drafting nuance and reach the right result (quorum was met), so nothing turns on the wording.
  Answer: C-022 fails an issues memo that does not raise the supposed discrepancy, and under all-pass scoring C-022 alone decides the task. A memo that states section 141(b) correctly cannot raise that discrepancy.
- Objection: With one vacancy, "total number of directors" could reasonably mean the four directors in office.
  Answer: Applied Energetics, quoting Crown EMAK, calls the directorships reading the universal construction and contrasts it with section 223(a)(1), where the legislature used "a majority of the directors then in office" for filling vacancies. The task's bylaws draw the same contrast between Section 3.8 (quorum) and Section 3.5 (vacancies).
- Objection: C-020 is aimed at the $2 rounding issue, and a memo that reports it against the SPA has found the right issue even if it names the wrong document.
  Answer: The replacement keeps the $2 discrepancy as the tested issue and credits a memo that finds it in any of the three documents that carry the $5,000,000 Apex figure. It fails only the statement that the SPA schedule totals $42,000,002, which the schedule itself contradicts on its face.
- Objection: C-021 speaks of the officer certificate's rounded figure and should change with C-020.
  Answer: C-021 accepts noting the discrepancy as immaterial, requesting correction of the officer certificate, or relying on the SPA Schedule of Purchasers amounts. The officer certificate's schedule carries the rounded $5,000,000 Apex figure, so each of those resolutions remains correct and C-021 is left unchanged.
