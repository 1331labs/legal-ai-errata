Twelve contract and policy templates published as `templates/<name>/template.md`, each derived from a Word original in `docx-originals/` by `scripts/convert_docx.py`; the README describes the markdown as LLM-optimized and marks fields to customise with `<mark>` tags.

## How corrections are accepted

Pull request or issue. The repository is CC0 1.0 and has no contribution guide in force. The Word originals are binary, so corrections to them are given as old and new text.
