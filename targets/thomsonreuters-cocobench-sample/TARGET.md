`cocobench-sample` is a public sample of CoCoBench, described in its README as Thomson Reuters Labs' standard for developing and evaluating agentic AI on legal work. It contains five tasks under `tests/` (`2-005`, `2-059`, `2-099`, `4-050`, `PLHO20260120-3-032`), each a `test.json` with the fields test_id, category, subcategory, task, prompt, documents, rubric (essential, donts, nice_to_have), ideal_answer_type and ideal_answer, plus source PDFs for four of them. The README states that the full benchmark has grown past 2,100 tasks and is proprietary.
